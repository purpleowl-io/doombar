4. Slack channels

Channels, DMs, and group DMs you are in. Checked ones get a row in the Slack panel.

Channels with no message in this many days are left off the panel. 0 shows them all.

5. Email senders

One per line. acme matches any domain with that label (acme.com, mail.acme.io); globex.com matches that domain and its subdomains; bob@x.com matches one address. Long unread prospect mail is summarised automatically.

6. Calendars & colours

Checked calendars are merged into the Calendar panel; the colour marks their events.
