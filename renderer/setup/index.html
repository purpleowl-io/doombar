Doombar setup

Tokens are tested before they are stored, then encrypted with the OS credential store.

2. Slack

Create the app from the manifest at (Create New App → From a manifest → paste slack-manifest.json). Generate an app-level token with connections:write, then Install to Workspace and copy both OAuth tokens.

3. Mail & calendar accounts

Any number of Google and Microsoft 365 accounts. Their mail and calendars are merged in the panels; a message or meeting that reaches two accounts shows once. Untick Mail or Calendar to leave an account out of that panel; the label tags its rows when several accounts are shown.

Microsoft 365 app

One multi-tenant app registration serves every Microsoft account. Register automatically signs you in once and creates an app called Doombar in your directory (needs an account allowed to register apps). To do it by hand instead: → New registration, any organisation + personal accounts, platform Mobile and desktop with redirect http://localhost, allow public client flows; paste its Application (client) ID. Accounts in other organisations may need their admin to approve Doombar.

Dashboard

What the panels show. Saved into config.json in the data folder; a running dashboard picks changes up within a second.

4. Slack channels

Channels, DMs, and group DMs you are in. Checked ones get a row in the Slack panel.

Channels with no message in this many days are left off the panel. 0 shows them all.

5. Email senders

One per line. acme matches any domain with that label (acme.com, mail.acme.io); globex.com matches that domain and its subdomains; bob@x.com matches one address. Long unread prospect mail is summarised automatically.

6. Calendars & colours

Checked calendars, from every account, are merged into the Calendar panel; the colour marks their events. After loading, an account with nothing checked is left out of the panel; a newly added account shows its main calendar until you choose.

7. Theme, visualizer & unread tint

Tapping the spectrum on the strip also cycles through these, then album art, then back.

Amber when something arrives, red by the first time, then a slow blend between two reds. Reading clears it.
